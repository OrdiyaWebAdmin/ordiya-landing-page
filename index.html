Start anywhere.
ORDIYA takes it from there.

The good stuff rarely begins in a perfect form. It starts on a sticky note, in a notebook, or as something you say before you forget. ORDIYA lets you review the captured text or transcript, choose where it belongs, and save it locally.

Capture your way

Write it. Snap it. Say it.

Use the input that fits the moment, then review the title, date, time, and captured content before saving.

Type it.

Start with a reminder, appointment, list, or idea. Choose a date, shape the details, and save it where you expect to find it.

Snap it.

Photograph a handwritten note, sticky note, or short list. Review the extracted text and confirm the details before saving.

Say it.

Speak a reminder or idea when typing is inconvenient. Review the transcript, choose the date, and save the thought while it is fresh.

One simple review loop

Photograph the note. Check what ORDIYA found.

The current photo flow keeps you in control: capture a note, read the extracted text, correct anything uncertain, confirm the date and time, then save.
